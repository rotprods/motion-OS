# Brand Commercial Grammar Examples

These examples preserve the structural knowledge supplied for training/evaluation. They are not default templates and must not cause cross-brand leakage.

## Duolingo-derived example

15 s, 16:9, hyper-commercial gamified grammar. Approximate one-second beats:
notification hook → lesson select → question blitz → XP reward → streak pressure → leaderboard ascent → badge unlock → mascot hero → language world → hearts pressure → progress → calm micro-habit hold → reward systems condense → stable logo end card.

Key learnings:
- Product semantics are motion primitives: XP count, streak, hearts, leaderboard, lesson completion.
- Reward animation must be mechanically readable.
- Mascot is a hero asset with anatomy/integrity gates.
- Fast pacing still needs one hero focus per beat.
- A calm 1 s hold before final acceleration increases contrast.
- End card settles for 1.5–2 s with zero drift.

## Spotify-derived example

15 s, 16:9, hyper-commercial audio grammar. Approximate one-second beats:
player boot → now-playing expansion → playlist cascade → discovery scroll → save toggle → lyrics reveal → audio pulse space → artist profile → stats burst → multi-device flow → cover wall → calm player hold → modules condense → stable logo end card.

Key learnings:
- Audio is not accompaniment; it is part of the motion graph.
- Player controls and progress must behave plausibly.
- Waveforms/pulses require beat synchronization rather than decorative randomness.
- UI proof, culture/cover art and emotional holds can coexist in one commercial grammar.
- Lyrics and stats have stricter text-readability constraints than decorative imagery.
- Final logo card must be stable and iconic.

## Cross-example abstractions

1. `product_semantics → primitive selection`: choose primitives from what users actually do in the product.
2. `brand energy → pacing policy`: gamified/audio products can support ~1 s beat switching; Apple-premium desktop generally cannot.
3. `contrast beat`: intentional calm after sustained high energy.
4. `condense_to_brand`: previous modules collapse into a single final brand anchor.
5. `proof before spectacle`: authentic UI/product behavior is more important than decorative effects.
6. `hard text gate`: text never participates in destructive morph/blur.
7. `final hold`: final memory frame is a separate beat with no post-settle drift.
